# Proposal comparison

All three use the existing product and the same coverage distinction. They are
static responsive composition studies; their links lead to the functioning local
Astro implementation. They do not pretend to be three separately built map engines.

| Direction | Composition and typography | Strength | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [City Desk](city-desk/index.html) | Bounded left evidence rail, large map; grotesk UI | Best entry into both place and evidence | Less editorial drama |
| [City Edition](city-edition/index.html) | Wide serif masthead, map and entries side by side | Strong first-visit story | More scrolling before exploration |
| [Field Instrument](field-instrument/index.html) | Dark utility frame, wide model, horizontal project deck; mono titles | Strong repeat-use map focus | Weakest explanation for newcomers |

**Recommendation: City Desk.** It makes the credible model the main visual while
keeping the next action readable. Implemented in web/src, including the index and
shared dossier typography. Agent-selected; not claimed as owner approval.

The production implementation adapts the study to the existing overlay map controls,
phone viewport and evidence components. Alternatives remain preserved here.
