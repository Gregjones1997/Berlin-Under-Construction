# German-language workflow and coverage expansion

**Owner direction recorded:** 8 September 2026. Planning only; no new verification,
project coverage or extraction run is claimed by this document.

## Outcome

Build a German-centered construction product despite the author's inability to
speak German, and demonstrate the accuracy achieved together with the bounded
amount of German-speaking colleague work required. The workflow feeds the actual
construction app. The next coverage target is ten evidence-backed projects,
including map placement where location evidence permits it.

Finding an independent German-speaking verifier has been the largest bottleneck.
The owner wants a free route forward. Paid review is not a prerequisite for
preparation or implementation. Human authority remains necessary for verified
contextual meanings and eligible reference answers under ADR-008 and ADR-015.
This plan changes work order, not publication or golden-set rules.

## Deliverables in order

1. Inventory the terms and passages actually used by the three pilots. Link each
   to its source, glossary version, app field and current verification status.
   Separate exact wording checks from interpretation decisions. Do not populate
   golden answers or select contextual dictionary senses on a human's behalf.
2. Prepare a small review pack: German passages with sufficient context, source
   links, proposed glossary mappings clearly labelled as unverified, and blank
   reference-answer fields. Include uncertainty, object and phase scope, dates
   and ambiguous cases. The human must be able to reject a proposed mapping.
3. Count review entries, German source words and passages. Deduplicate repeated
   terms without dropping distinct contexts. Estimate colleague time using the
   method below; no invented measured rate or promised saving.
4. Research seven additional candidate projects and record evidence availability,
   location support, vocabulary reuse and new contextual questions. Ten projects
   is a target, not permission to fill gaps. Prepare private proposals and tooling
   while a verifier is unavailable; publish only eligible facts under existing
   evidence and review rules.
5. Integrate the vocabulary with the app's display path. Preserve canonical German,
   source evidence, version and verification status; show proposed English glosses
   only where permitted by the existing display contract. Keep contested meanings
   unresolved. Expose an inspectable glossary and concrete source-to-display examples.
6. When human verification is available, freeze eligible reference answers before
   scoring. Keep evaluation examples separate from prompt development. Report
   correct interpretations, errors, omissions and review referrals with denominators
   and sample limits, alongside actual colleague time. Publish the resulting case
   study and demonstrate the expanded app.

## Estimating colleague effort

Report initial glossary verification separately from per-project passage review
and exception resolution. Count source words including the context the reviewer
must read, and distinguish unique words in the pack from repeated review workload.
Word count alone cannot capture ambiguity.

Planning time = setup minutes + (entries × assumed minutes per entry) +
(routine passages × assumed minutes per passage) +
(exceptions × assumed minutes per exception).

Use non-overlapping review categories to avoid double counting. Publish the
counts, low/high assumptions and resulting range. Label the range as an
unvalidated planning estimate. A timed small sample should replace assumed rates
when possible, recording reading, correction and exception time. Record reviewer
competence and verification scope without publicly naming a natural person.

## Evidence of success

- Ten eligible project dossiers are reachable, with supported map positions and
  explicit missing-location states where necessary.
- Users can inspect real German-to-display examples and the glossary entries
  behind them, including unresolved interpretations and verification status.
- Vocabulary coverage and interpretation accuracy are reported separately. A
  matching term, exact quotation or passing test does not establish meaning.
- An independent human verifies the relevant glossary scope and reference values;
  only those eligible values support scored evaluation.
- Review workload and time are reported, separating estimates from observations
  and one-time setup from recurring effort. No efficiency gain is claimed without
  a measured comparison.

Until verification is available, report the remaining review workload precisely
and continue independent preparation. Do not describe the language problem as
solved merely because the glossary or demonstration exists. No model-provider
calls are authorized by this plan.
