### Resolution of the relative anchor

`Schuljahresbeginn 2026/27` resolves against the Berlin Ferienordnung:

`Sommerferien Do. 09.07.2026 - Sa. 22.08.2026` — with the first school day
after the break given as `Mo. 24.08.2026`.

**Resolved date: Monday 24 August 2026.** Authority: SenBJF Ferienordnung für
das Land Berlin 2024/2025 bis 2029/2030. The anchor wording remains the
canonical stored value; the resolved date is derived and carries the resolving
authority.

**Direction of movement.** With the anchor resolved, the December 2023 target
(~24 August 2026) is **earlier** than the current target (31 August 2026). The
movement is a slip of approximately one week, not an acceleration. The
July 2024 statement (`im Herbst 2026`) is later than both the target before it
and the target after it, and is the outlier in the sequence.

**Owner interpretation, 2026-08-06:** given a school project delivered for the
2026/27 year, a completion target falling within one week of the school-year
start is read as consistent with the original intent. Recorded as
`owner-judgment` at the interpretation layer. The two dates remain stored as
distinct claims; they are not collapsed. Note that the on-schedule status does
not depend on this interpretation — Drucksache 19/26230 asserts it directly.

**Residual open question:** whether `Schuljahresbeginn` denotes the first
teaching day (24 August) or a formal school-year start (1 August in several
Länder). The Ferienordnung gives teaching days, not the legal year boundary.
The gap changes the size of the slip, not its direction.

## Financial measures

### ⚠ QUARANTINED — construction cost, scope conflict unresolved

**Drucksache 19/18429**, `Anlage 2 zur Frage 2`, headed `Geplante Schulneubauvorhaben`, dated `11.03.2024`. Row verbatim:

`III   02Y03, Heinrich-Hertz-Gymnasium: Ersatzneubau mit Sporthalle; 10243, Lange Straße 60   107.300.000   752*   2026   x   x   6.171.259,31`

Column headers verbatim:

`Maßnahmenbezeichnung | BSO Tranche | Gesamtkosten (€) | Schulplätze | Fertigstellungsjahr | Compartment | Mischform | Grundstückskauf | Mittelabflüsse bis 31.12.23 (€)`

Scope footnote verbatim:

`Anlagen 1 und 2: Kosten für Außenanlagen sowie Sporthallen sind Bestandteil der Gesamtkosten einer Baumaßnahme. Kosten der Grundstückskäufe gehören nicht zu den Gesamtkosten.`

Places footnote verbatim:

`* baulich geschaffene Schulplätze als Ersatz von Bestandsschulplätzen`

**Why quarantined.** The measure is named `Ersatzneubau mit Sporthalle` and the
footnote states sports-hall costs are inside `Gesamtkosten`. The Bezirksamt
states the sports hall does not fit on the plot and is built separately on
Fredersdorfer Straße by SenSBW. Two official sources describe different scopes
under one project label.

**Handling, per the owner's ruling of 2026-08-06:** the value is stored with
its span, source and scope string and marked **unverified**. It does not render
as the project's cost. The card shows that a figure exists and that its scope
is unresolved. It is never silently dropped and never silently adopted.

Two further conflicts from the same row:

- `752*` places against HOWOGE's `2-4 zügiges Gymnasium mit 784 Schulplätzen`. The asterisk footnote explains part of this (replacement places versus total capacity) but does not reconcile the figures.
- `Fertigstellungsjahr 2026` is a bare table field with no modal, as at March 2024.

### Delivery model — why there may be no simple construction budget

The project is delivered under the Berliner Schulbauoffensive
Erbbaurecht-and-Mietvertrag model. Bezirksamt PM Nr. 301, 2023-12-21:

`Kernbestandteil der Einbeziehung der HOWOGE in die Berliner Schulbauoffensive ist der Abschluss von Erbbaurechts- und Mietverträgen zwischen den Bezirken, in denen die HOWOGE im Schulbau aktiv ist, und der HOWOGE selbst.`

`Die nächsten Schritte sind die Erteilung der Baugenehmigung, die Zustimmung zu den langjährigen Verpflichtungsermächtigungen für die Mietzahlungen, die die Senatsverwaltung für Finanzen erteilen muss, und final die Zustimmung des Abgeordnetenhauses.`

The public financial trace for this project is `Verpflichtungsermächtigungen`
for rent payments, not a construction budget. The specific VE amounts are
**not checked** — a Hauptausschuss search for the Erbbaurecht/Mietvertrag
approval is the next financial step.

## Why this pilot is valuable

C-010 produced five distinct completion or handover terms across four
publishing bodies for one project, a cost figure that cannot be used because of
a silent scope inclusion, a relative date anchor that required a statutory
instrument to resolve, and a source that contradicts itself on roles within a
single page. Every one of those is a distinct failure mode the extraction
pipeline must survive.
