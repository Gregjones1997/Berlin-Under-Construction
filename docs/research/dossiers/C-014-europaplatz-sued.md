### Naming note — not a source error

The project page reads `Der Europaplatz Süd als nördlicher Vorplatz des
Berliner Hauptbahnhofs`. "Süd" and "nördlicher" are consistent: Europaplatz as
a whole is the station's **northern** forecourt, and Europaplatz Süd is the
**southern portion of Europaplatz**. Recorded so this is not repeatedly
re-flagged as a contradiction.

### Unresolved reading, 2023-06-26

In `Unter Beteiligung einer Jury mit Fachexpertinnen und – experten soll bis Herbst 2023 ein Konzept ausgewählt werden, das zur Grundlage der nachfolgenden Umgestaltung des Europaplatzes Süd bis Ende 2025 dient.` the modal `soll` governs `ein Konzept ausgewählt werden`; `bis Ende 2025` attaches to `der nachfolgenden Umgestaltung`. ### ⚠ Unreconciled — the project page figure

The project page states, under the field label `Gesamtkosten:`, the value:

`finanziert aus dem Plätzeprogramm: 1.900.000 €`

Two distinct problems, both recorded, neither resolved:

1. **Field label and value assert different financial types.** The label says
   total costs; the value says financing from the squares programme. Storing
   this as `Gesamtkosten` would introduce a type error originating in the
   source's own layout.
2. **The figure matches, to the euro, a programme-level budget line** in the
   Hauptausschuss paper for `Kapitel 2712, Titel 72004 – Umgestaltung von
   Stadtplätzen`, which funds the whole squares programme:
   `Verpflichtungsermächtigungen 2026 (Entwurf): 1.900.000,00 €`

**The exact match is recorded as a documented coincidence of figures. The causal
claim — that the project page displays a programme-wide commitment
authorization as one project's total cost — is not asserted and requires an
owner ruling.** Per the owner's ruling of 2026-08-06 this value renders with an
uncertainty affordance rather than being suppressed or silently corrected. It
is the first test case for the conflict marker's **unreconciled** state.

## Accountability finding

The project page states a completion year with no publication date, no `Stand`,
no version history, and no archive snapshot. No member of the public can
determine whether that `2026` was written in 2023 or last week. The same page
carries a cost figure whose type its own field label contradicts.

Recorded as a candidate feature rather than only a research gap: a
**"last verifiable as at"** field per claim, where absence is displayed rather
than hidden. This is the product's thesis appearing in the evidence.
