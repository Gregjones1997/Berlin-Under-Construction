# C-010 post-date milestone recheck

**Checked:** 2026-09-03

**Fact:** `c010-technical-handover-current`

**Status:** The 31 August date has passed. A newer official source moves the
handover wording to early September; no checked source explicitly confirms that
the handover has occurred.

## Sources checked

1. The [25 August 2026 district update](https://www.berlin.de/ba-friedrichshain-kreuzberg/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/2026/pressemitteilung.1706600.php)
   states: `Der Neubau des Heinrich-Hertz-Gymnasiums für 784 Schüler*innen am
   Standort Lange Straße in unmittelbarer Nähe des Ostbahnhofs in Friedrichshain
   wird Anfang September 2026 von der HOWOGE an das Bezirksamt übergeben.` This
   is later than the source currently displayed by the site. It does not say
   that the handover has already happened.
2. The [24 August 2026 Senate event notice](https://www.berlin.de/sen/bjf/service/presse/pressearchiv-2026/pressemitteilung.1706278.php)
   schedules a 2 September event and building tour in the new school building.
   An event at the site is not treated as proof of technical handover.
3. The [current HOWOGE project page](https://unternehmen.howoge.de/schulbau/heinrich-hertz-gymnasium-projektuebersicht.html),
   observed 3 September 2026, still presents the project at `Im Bau`, gives
   `Bauzeit 2024 bis 2026`, and states `Die Endfertigstellung ist für 2026
   vorgesehen.` It does not confirm the handover as completed.

## Display decision

Keep the existing exact 19 June planned statement, its source date and its
`geplant` qualifier. Because the date has passed, the deterministic display
warning states that no confirming source is recorded and does not assert
completion.

Do not add the newer `Anfang September 2026` statement to the public projection
without the required owner review of the new value. The recheck establishes
that the older date is historical evidence, not a current completion claim.

## Method and limit

This was a retrieval and verbatim-span check against official primary sources.
No agent interpretation of the German wording enters the golden set or a new
accepted public claim. The absence of an explicit confirmation in the checked
sources is not evidence that the handover did or did not occur.
